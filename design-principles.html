Designprinciples

Eleven principles for any design, from the first sketch to the finished build. The first ten are qualities to hold every part to. The eleventh, overall quality, is what you get when you do.

  1. Simplicity
  2. Strength
  3. Flexibility
  4. Reliability
  5. Economy
  6. Predictability
  7. Performance
  8. Security
  9. Maintainability
  10. Accessibility
  11. Overall Quality

Simplicity

Simplicity means fewer moving parts, fewer processes and fewer inputs. As the number of parts and processes increases, so does the probability of a mistake. Most mistakes are made when an overly complex system is forced to fit into a small space.

Strength

Strength of design relates to the forces that it is capable of withstanding. Without sufficient strength, a design lacks durability and resilience. The stronger the design, the more stresses it will tolerate.

Back to contents

Flexibility

Flexibility is the ability to respond to change. If a design is not flexible, it will be brittle. A flexible design is one that can adapt to new circumstances and permits modifications.

Back to contents

Reliability

Reliability is the ability to continue working under varying conditions. Reliability is related to strength and flexibility. A design without strength is not long-lasting and a design without flexibility has less ability to adapt; therefore, it will be unreliable when conditions change.

Back to contents

Economy

Economy refers to a design that requires less resources and is scalable. With fewer necessary resources, the overall cost is lower, reducing waste and assuring affordability. Economy of scale means that as the system grows, and increases units in production, the lower the cost per unit of output.

Back to contents

Predictability

Predictability is the capability of the design to perform consistently over time. Predictability is also related to intuitiveness. The more predictable the design, the more likely it is that the design will perform as people expect.

Back to contents

Performance

Performance is the capability of the design to run smoothly under demand. If a design cannot perform at the extremes of its capabilities, it is likely that the system is flawed. The more performant the design, the more likely the design will run without lag or latency.

Back to contents

Security

Security is the capability of the design to resist attack. If a design is not secure, some people will take advantage of it and others will be negatively affected. The more secure the design, the more likely it is that the design will keep people safe and protect what they hold dear – whether it’s their data, money, belongings, family, or their own personal safety.

Back to contents

Maintainability

Maintainability is the ease with which a design can be changed, repaired, or upgraded. If a design is not maintainable, it may need to be replaced.

Back to contents

Accessibility

Accessibility is the capability of the design to be used by those who need it. The more accessible the design is, the more likely it will be used widely.

Back to contents

Overall Quality

When these design principles have been thoughtfully applied, before and during implementation, the resulting design is more likely to possess overall integrity. The capability of the design to perform its purpose is assured because each part is held to a high standard, reflecting its level of quality.

In short

Fewer moving parts, fewer processes and fewer inputs: that is simplicity. A design’s strength is the force it is capable of withstanding. The ability to respond to change is flexibility. When conditions vary, reliability is the ability to keep working. A design that needs fewer resources and scales well has economy. Over time, predictability means it performs consistently. Under demand, performance is the capability to run smoothly. To resist attack, a design needs security. Maintainability is the ease with which it can be changed, repaired or upgraded. And when those who need it can use it, the design has accessibility. When these design principles have been thoughtfully applied, before and during implementation, the resulting design is more likely to possess overall integrity.

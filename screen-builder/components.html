Every component in Screen Builder, in one place

Screen Builder is Dlightning's no-code tool for mocking up mobile app screens: drag ready-made components onto a phone screen, restyle them and click through them as a prototype. This page is its full catalog. It shows every component and each of its variants, drawn from the same code the builder uses, so what you see here is what lands on your screen.

Open the builder
  • Try your colors. Pick a primary, secondary and accent color in the header to see every component in your palette.
  • Switch icon styles between solid, outline, fill and duotone, or turn on dark mode.
  • Find a component with the filter box or the category links, and zoom out to compare more at once.

Every template in Screen Builder, in one place

Screen Builder is Dlightning's no-code tool for mocking up mobile app screens. Templates give you a finished screen in one click, and app packs (Pro) build a whole app at once, its screens already connected so you can click through them as a prototype. This page shows them all, drawn from the same data the builder's template picker uses.

Open the builder
  • Try your colors. Pick a primary, secondary and accent color in the header to see every template in your palette.
  • Switch icon styles between solid, outline, fill and duotone, or turn on dark mode.
  • Hover a screen to scroll through it. Find one with the filter box or the category links, and zoom out to compare more at once.

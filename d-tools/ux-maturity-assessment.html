          Frequently asked questions

          How long does the UX maturity assessment take?

          About two to three minutes — seven multiple-choice questions, with instant results.

          Is the assessment free?

          Yes. It's free, needs no signup, and gives you a maturity level plus recommendations right away.

          How is the UX maturity score calculated?

          Each question scores 1–4. Your answers total into a percentage mapped to one of four levels: Early Journey, Growing Foundation, Advanced Practitioner, or Experience Design Leader.

          Who should take this assessment?

          Design leaders, product managers, founders, and teams who want an honest read on their UX practice and a clear next step.

          How can we improve our UX maturity?

          Start with the recommendations for your level, then advance one dimension at a time. Our Experience Builder helps you put those practices into action.

          How it works

          1. Answer 7 questions, one for each dimension of UX practice.
          2. Each answer places that dimension at one of four stages.
          3. See your overall score, your stage and your full profile.
          4. Get next steps aimed at your two weakest dimensions.
          5. Save your results as a PDF or PNG and retake it next quarter.

          The assessment covers research practices, design decisions, journey thinking, measurement, collaboration, iteration and behavioral insight. Your answers stay in your browser.

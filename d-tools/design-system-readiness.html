      How it works

      1. Answer three questions about need: team size, product surface, and how far your UI has drifted.
      2. Answer four about capacity: process, design–dev partnership, time and ownership, and leadership support.
      3. See where you land on the readiness map, with a score for each part.
      4. Get the one capacity gap to close first, and an approach and timeline sized to your team.
      5. Follow a four-release roadmap from v0.1 to v1.0, and save it all as a PDF or PNG.

      Most design systems that fail were needed. They fail because nobody had the time, the process or the backing to keep them current. This assessment scores need and capacity separately so you can see which one is holding you back. Your answers stay in your browser.

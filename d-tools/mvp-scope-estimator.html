                  MVP cost and scope, briefly

                  What the estimate above is built on, if you want to check it or adjust it.

                  How much does an MVP cost?

                  For a focused v1 built by a freelancer or small studio, often somewhere between $25,000 and $150,000. Three things move it most: how many features are in v1, whether the screens are designed before building starts, and how sure you are that the features are the right ones. No-code tools can bring it well under that, at the cost of flexibility later.

                  How long does it take to build an MVP?

                  Two to four months of work is common for a focused v1 built by an experienced developer. A studio can shorten the calendar time by running people in parallel, but not the total hours you pay for.

                  What should an MVP include?

                  What someone needs to get the main result once, and to pay you if you charge. Admin panels, dashboards, search and team permissions make a product easier to run at scale, and can usually be handled by hand or with an off-the-shelf tool until you have the customers who need them.

                  Why a range and not a price?

                  Because the features haven't been designed yet, and a single number would hide that. The range narrows as the details settle: finished designs and evidence from customers both bring the top end down. If you haven't talked to customers yet, the Customer Interview Script Builder writes the questions.

                  How it works

                  1. Pick what you're building: a web app, a mobile app, both, a marketplace or an internal tool.
                  2. Tick the features you have in mind. Don't trim yet; the tool shows which ones can usually wait.
                  3. Say how far along the design is and how sure you are about the feature list. Both change how wide the range is.
                  4. Say who will build it. That sets the hourly rate and how long it takes on the calendar.
                  5. Move features between v1 and later and watch the estimate change. Copy the scope, download it, or send it to us for a proposal.

                  Estimates are in weeks of work for an experienced developer, then multiplied by an hourly rate you can change. Your answers stay in your browser.

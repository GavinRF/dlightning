      How it works

      1. Pick the tendency that blocks you most: over-optimizing, over-planning, or over-protecting.
      2. Name what it's costing you and the problem space you know deeply.
      3. Pick the broken workflow you've seen up close. It becomes the Pain in your positioning line.
      4. Type your Mechanism and Outcome to finish a Pain → Mechanism → Outcome statement.
      5. Commit to one move, dated a week out, and save the sheet as a PDF or PNG.

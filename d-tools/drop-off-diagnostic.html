          How it works

          1. Pick the stage where you lose the most people: visit, sign up, first use, coming back, or paying.
          2. Say how you know. A hunch and a measured funnel lead to different first steps.
          3. Answer three questions about that stage: what you see in the product, what users say, and what the numbers show.
          4. Each answer points at one of four common causes for that stage. You get the top three, ranked, each with a check you can run in ten minutes.
          5. Leave with the number to track, a test for the top cause, and the research that would confirm it. Save it all as a PDF or PNG.

          Most products don't lose people everywhere at once. They lose them at one stage, for one or two reasons. Finding which one before you redesign saves you fixing the parts that already work. Your answers stay in your browser.

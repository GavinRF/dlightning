    Frequently asked questions

    How do I validate an AI agent idea before building it?

    Deliver the outcome by hand first. Take a real customer's input, produce the output yourself, and hand it back where they already work. If they use it and ask for more, you've found a task worth automating. If they don't, a model won't change that.

    What makes an AI agent idea strong?

    A specific person with a trigger that makes the pain urgent, a repetitive task with a clear finished output, and evidence that people already pay to get it done. The quiz scores those three: who it's for, problem fit, and market proof.

    Is it bad if nobody sells anything like my agent yet?

    Not necessarily, but it's a question to answer before building. Find out where the money and time go today, even if the answer is a spreadsheet and an intern. That's what your agent competes with.
